# Sacred Visions

Sacred Visions is the curated encounter layer of the Arcanean Library. It
selects and sequences source passages, visual meditations, sound, commentary,
and original responses. It is not a separate repository today.

Its upstream evidence lives in [`research/sacred-texts`](../../research/sacred-texts/README.md).
Its downstream original mythology and scripture live in [`content`](../../content/).

## Experience unit

Every published vision should contain:

- one verifiable primary witness in its source language;
- one licensed or newly commissioned translation, when needed;
- one short historical frame;
- one irreducible question;
- one original visual or sonic interpretation;
- one accountable Arcanea response clearly labeled as new composition;
- source and rights links that remain visible from the experience.

## What this collection refuses

- quote-card spirituality detached from works and editions;
- anonymous “ancient wisdom”;
- flattening incompatible traditions into one universal claim;
- generated imitation of a named sacred author;
- modern translations presented as public-domain originals;
- aestheticized suffering without historical or ethical context.
