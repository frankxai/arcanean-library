# Sacred Texts Canon Contract

## Architectural decision

`Sacred Texts` and `Sacred Visions` are layers inside `arcanean-library`, not
new top-level brands or repositories.

| Layer | Owns | Must never claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `research/sacred-texts` | witnesses, originals, editions, source and rights records | that editorial selection is revelation |
| `collections/sacred-visions` | curation, sequence, comparison, commentary, contemplative experience | that interpretation is verbatim source |
| `content` | original Arcanea codices, myths, prayers, poems, rituals | that new fiction was written by a historical thinker |

A separate repository becomes justified only when the corpus acquires an
independent release cycle, storage profile, permission boundary, or scholarly
governance team. Naming alone is not a boundary.

## Epistemic labels

Every public surface must distinguish:

- `primary-text`
- `historical-context`
- `scholarly-interpretation`
- `tradition-claim`
- `editorial-selection`
- `new-composition`
- `practice`
- `empirical-claim`

Contemplative testimony is represented as testimony. Theology is represented
as theology. Metaphor does not become physics, and neuroscience does not become
proof of metaphysics.

## Fidelity rules

1. Preserve the source language beside any translation.
2. Name translator, edition, date, and stable location.
3. Record editorial modernization of spelling and punctuation.
4. Never repair a source silently. Use brackets, notes, or a separate normalized
   witness.
5. Do not manufacture composite quotations.
6. Do not write in the simulated voice of a named thinker. Extract craft
   principles, then compose in an accountable Arcanea or FrankX voice.
7. Treat disputed attribution as data, not inconvenience.

## Comparative rule

Comparison must preserve difference. Similar language about emptiness,
detachment, union, attention, surrender, or darkness does not establish an
identical metaphysics. Each comparison records:

- the source tradition's own term;
- what problem the term answers;
- what practice or doctrine surrounds it;
- where the analogy breaks.

## Sacred Visions selection test

A passage enters the curated collection only when it has:

- textual beauty that survives slow reading aloud;
- conceptual force beyond decorative spirituality;
- a verifiable source location;
- a distinct role in the sequence;
- a rights-safe publication mode;
- commentary that clarifies without domesticating the source.
